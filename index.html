MACHINE LEARNING • TIME SERIES • STACKED LSTM

Stock Market Prediction & Trend Analysis

Completed

Problem

Financial market price movements are volatile and non-linear, requiring robust historical time-series analytics and deep sequential modeling to capture temporal dependencies.

Approach

Ingested 10+ years of Google (GOOG) historical financial data via yfinance. Preprocessed data with MinMax scaling, computed 100/200-day moving averages (MA100/MA200), and built a 100-step sliding lookback window.

Neural Architecture

Engineered a 4-layer Stacked LSTM network (50 → 60 → 80 → 120 units) with sequential Dropout regularization (0.2–0.5) to capture long-term trends and forecast future closing prices.
